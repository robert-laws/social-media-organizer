Here's a distinction worth making. A prompt trick is the phrasing that coaxes a good answer out of today's AI. Judgment is knowing whether that answer is true, whether the tool fit the task, where it might quietly mislead you.

The trick expires with the next model update. The judgment lasts a career. Libraries have taught the second one for generations, we just called it information literacy. What do you think AI instruction should focus on?
