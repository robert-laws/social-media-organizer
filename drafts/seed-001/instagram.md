Two things get confused in every AI workshop, and the difference matters.

The first is a prompt trick: the exact phrasing that gets a good answer out of this month's model. The second is judgment: knowing whether the answer is true, whether the tool was the right one, where it would quietly mislead you.

The first expires with the next update. The second lasts a career.

Libraries have taught the second one for decades. We called it information literacy. AI just gives it a new surface.

Share this with the librarian planning next semester's instruction.
